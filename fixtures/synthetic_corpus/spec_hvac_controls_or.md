# Manufacturer Specification: Building Automation and VAV Controls Materials Specification

## Overview

Manufacturer specifications and unit costs for BACnet controller, VAV controller, temperature sensor, CO2 sensor.

This document lists manufacturer materials and unit costs for a building automation and vav controls project in Portland, OR. All prices are synthetic and for testing only.

## Materials

Items covered: BACnet controller, VAV controller, temperature sensor, CO2 sensor.

- BACnet building controller, 64 point: $2,850.00 each. One per building.
- VAV box controller, BACnet MS/TP with actuator: $395.00 each. One per VAV box.
- Room temperature sensor, BACnet: $68.00 each. One per zone.
- CO2 sensor, wall mount, 0 to 2000 ppm: $210.00 each. One per densely occupied zone.
- Duct temperature sensor: $42.00 each. One per VAV discharge.
- 22 AWG shielded twisted pair, 1000 ft spool: $165.00 per spool. One spool per 8 controllers.

## Labor

- Controls technician: 4 hours per VAV controller.
- Electrician: 1.5 hours per controller.
