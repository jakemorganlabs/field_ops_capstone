# Residential Solar Installation Proposal

## Project Summary

Synthetic proposal for a residential solar installation in San Diego, CA.

## Scope

Install a 8 kW DC rooftop solar array with string inverter and monitoring.

## Materials

- 400 W monocrystalline solar panel: $285.00 each. Need 20 panels.
- 8 kW string inverter: $1,850.00 each. Need 1 inverter.
- Racking system, flush mount: $1,250.00 per array. Need 1 system.
- 10 AWG PV wire, 500 ft spool: $245.00 per spool. Need 1 spool.
- Roof penetration flashing: $18.00 each. Need 20 flashings.
- Monitoring gateway: $245.00 each. Need 1 gateway.

## Labor

- Solar installer: 8 hours per kW at $85.00 per hour.
- Electrician: 8 hours total at $82.00 per hour.
