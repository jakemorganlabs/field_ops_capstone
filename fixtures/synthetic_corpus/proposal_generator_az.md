# Backup Generator Installation Proposal

## Project Summary

Synthetic proposal for a standby generator installation in Phoenix, AZ.

## Scope

Install a 48 kW natural gas standby generator with automatic transfer switch.

## Materials

- 48 kW standby generator, natural gas: $14,500.00 each. Need 1 generator.
- 200 A automatic transfer switch: $2,850.00 each. Need 1 switch.
- 2-inch gas pipe, 10 ft: $42.00 each. Need 20 sticks.
- Gas shutoff valve: $85.00 each. Need 2 valves.
- Concrete pad, precast: $450.00 each. Need 1 pad.
- Battery, 12 V: $145.00 each. Need 1 battery.

## Labor

- Electrician: 24 hours at $76.00 per hour.
- Plumber: 8 hours at $92.00 per hour.
