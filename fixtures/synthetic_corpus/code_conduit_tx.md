# Code Reference for EMT Conduit and Fittings in TX

This code reference applies to emt conduit and fittings installations in Austin, TX. It summarizes the governing requirements for estimating and installation. Synthetic summary for testing only.

## Governing Codes

Code requirements covered: NEC Article 358, grounding required. TX code reference.

- NEC Article 358 Electrical Metallic Tubing
- NEC Article 250 Grounding and Bonding

## Requirements

- EMT conduit must be supported within 3 feet of each box and at intervals not exceeding 10 feet per NEC Article 358.
- Grounding required: EMT is an acceptable equipment grounding conductor only when all EMT connectors and couplings are made up wrench tight.
- A grounding bushing is required at every concentric or eccentric knockout entry.
- Maximum of four quarter bends, 360 degrees total, between pull points.
- EMT must not be installed where subject to severe physical damage or in direct contact with earth.
- Device boxes must be sized for conductor fill per NEC 314.16.

## Labor Guidance

- Work must be performed by a licensed contractor with a permit and final inspection.
- Inspection and documentation time should be estimated at 5 percent of installation labor.
