# LED Lighting Retrofit Proposal

## Project Summary

Synthetic proposal for an LED lighting retrofit in Seattle, WA.

## Scope

Replace 120 existing fluorescent troffers with LED panels in an office building.

## Materials

- 2x4 LED troffer, 40 W, 4000 K: $125.00 each. Need 120 panels.
- LED driver, 40 W: $28.00 each. Need 120 drivers.
- Mounting hardware kit: $4.50 each. Need 120 kits.
- Wire, 14 AWG THHN, 500 ft spool: $95.00 per spool. Need 2 spools.
- Wire nut, 100-pack: $8.50 per pack. Need 2 packs.
- Occupancy sensor, ceiling mount: $65.00 each. Need 12 sensors.

## Labor

- Electrician: 0.5 hours per fixture at $80.00 per hour.
- Apprentice: 0.25 hours per fixture at $37.00 per hour.
