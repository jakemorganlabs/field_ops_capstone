# Government Facility CCTV Proposal

## Project Summary

Synthetic proposal for a CCTV system at a government facility in Washington, DC.

## Scope

Install 32 outdoor IP cameras with analytics, storage, and secure network.

## Materials

- 8 MP outdoor bullet camera: $485.00 each. Need 32 cameras.
- 64-channel NVR, 80 TB: $4,200.00 each. Need 1 NVR.
- 8 TB surveillance hard drive: $245.00 each. Need 10 drives.
- Fiber optic transceiver, single-mode: $125.00 each. Need 32 transceivers.
- 19-inch equipment rack: $685.00 each. Need 2 racks.
- Uninterruptible power supply, 3 kVA: $2,400.00 each. Need 1 UPS.

## Labor

- Security technician: 2 hours per camera at $85.00 per hour.
- Electrician: 1 hour per camera at $84.00 per hour.
