# Code Reference for Level 2 EV Charging Station in CO

This code reference applies to level 2 ev charging station installations in Denver, CO. It summarizes the governing requirements for estimating and installation. Synthetic summary for testing only.

## Governing Codes

Code requirements covered: NEC Article 625, ADA accessible. CO code reference.

- NEC Article 625 Electric Vehicle Power Transfer System
- 2010 ADA Standards for Accessible Design

## Requirements

- Each Level 2 charging station must be on an individual branch circuit sized at 125 percent of the charger rating per NEC Article 625.
- A 40 A charging station requires a 50 A breaker and 6 AWG THHN copper conductors.
- ADA accessible spaces must provide an accessible route to the charger and controls no higher than 48 inches above grade.
- Chargers mounted in a parking garage must be protected by a bollard mount or wheel stop against vehicle impact.
- Ventilation is not required for chargers listed for indoor use without ventilation.
- Load management or a load calculation per NEC 220 is required when six or more stations share a service.
