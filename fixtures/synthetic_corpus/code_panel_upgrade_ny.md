# Code Reference for 200 A Service Panel and Feeder in NY

This code reference applies to 200 a service panel and feeder installations in Buffalo, NY. It summarizes the governing requirements for estimating and installation. Synthetic summary for testing only.

## Governing Codes

Code requirements covered: NEC Article 110, utility coordination. NY code reference.

- NEC Article 110 Requirements for Electrical Installations
- NEC Article 230 Services

## Requirements

- Working space in front of the 200 A panel must be at least 36 inches deep and 30 inches wide per NEC Article 110.26.
- Utility coordination is required before the cutover; the utility must approve the meter socket and schedule the disconnect and reconnect.
- The 200 A feeder must use 2/0 AWG copper minimum at 75 degrees C terminations.
- Two ground rods spaced at least 6 feet apart are required unless one rod tests 25 ohms or less.
- All breakers must be listed for the panel and labeled with the load served.
- Available fault current must be marked on the service equipment per NEC 110.24.
