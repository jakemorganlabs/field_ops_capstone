# Data Center Cooling Upgrade Proposal

## Project Summary

Synthetic proposal for a data center cooling upgrade in Ashburn, VA.

## Scope

Add two in-row cooling units to an existing data center pod.

## Materials

- In-row air conditioner, 40 kW: $18,500.00 each. Need 2 units.
- Hot aisle containment door: $2,400.00 each. Need 4 doors.
- Containment roof panel: $685.00 each. Need 20 panels.
- Temperature sensor, rack mount: $35.00 each. Need 20 sensors.
- 3/4 inch copper refrigerant line, 50 ft coil: $145.00 per coil. Need 4 coils.
- Condensate pump: $185.00 each. Need 2 pumps.

## Labor

- HVAC technician: 16 hours per unit at $95.00 per hour.
- Electrician: 8 hours per unit at $83.00 per hour.
