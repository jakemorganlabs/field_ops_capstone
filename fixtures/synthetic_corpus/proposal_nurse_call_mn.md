# Nurse Call System Proposal

## Project Summary

Synthetic proposal for a nurse call system in Minneapolis, MN.

## Scope

Install a wireless nurse call system with 20 patient stations and 10 staff pendants.

## Materials

- Wireless patient station: $485.00 each. Need 20 stations.
- Staff pendant, wireless: $125.00 each. Need 10 pendants.
- Nurse call master station: $2,400.00 each. Need 1 master.
- Corridor dome light: $145.00 each. Need 10 lights.
- 18 AWG low voltage cable, 1000 ft spool: $115.00 per spool. Need 2 spools.
- Battery backup unit: $285.00 each. Need 2 units.

## Labor

- Low voltage technician: 1.5 hours per station at $75.00 per hour.
- Apprentice: 0.75 hours per station at $35.00 per hour.
