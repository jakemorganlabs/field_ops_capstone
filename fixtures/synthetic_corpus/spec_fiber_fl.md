# Manufacturer Specification: Radiation-Hardened Fiber Optic Trunk Materials Specification

## Overview

Manufacturer specifications and unit costs for radiation-hardened fiber, space-grade transceivers, vacuum-rated connectors.

This document lists manufacturer materials and unit costs for a radiation-hardened fiber optic trunk project in Kennedy Space Center, FL. All prices are synthetic and for testing only.

## Materials

Items covered: radiation-hardened fiber, space-grade transceivers, vacuum-rated connectors.

- Radiation-hardened single-mode fiber, 12-strand, 1000 ft reel: $2,450.00 per reel. One reel per 900 ft of trunk run.
- Space-grade optical transceiver, 10 Gb/s: $1,180.00 each. Two per link.
- Vacuum-rated fiber connector, hermetic feedthrough: $340.00 each. One per bulkhead penetration.
- Radiation-tolerant splice enclosure: $620.00 each. One per 300 ft.
- Fiber cleaning and inspection kit: $210.00 each. One per crew.

## Labor

- Fiber technician: 2.5 hours per splice point.
- Electrician: 1 hour per 100 ft of trunk.
