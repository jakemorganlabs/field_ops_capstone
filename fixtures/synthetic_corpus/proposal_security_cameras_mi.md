# Security Camera System Proposal

## Project Summary

Synthetic proposal for an IP security camera system in Detroit, MI.

## Scope

Install 24 IP cameras, network video recorder, and associated network cabling.

## Materials

- 4 MP IP dome camera: $285.00 each. Need 24 cameras.
- 32-channel NVR, 4 TB: $1,850.00 each. Need 1 NVR.
- 4 TB surveillance hard drive: $185.00 each. Need 4 drives.
- Cat6 cable, 1000 ft box: $165.00 per box. Need 3 boxes.
- PoE switch, 24 port: $685.00 each. Need 1 switch.
- Camera mounting bracket: $24.00 each. Need 24 brackets.

## Labor

- Low voltage technician: 1.5 hours per camera at $72.00 per hour.
- Apprentice: 0.5 hours per camera at $34.00 per hour.
