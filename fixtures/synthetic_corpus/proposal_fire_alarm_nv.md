# Fire Alarm System Upgrade Proposal

## Project Summary

Synthetic proposal for a fire alarm system upgrade in Las Vegas, NV.

## Scope

Replace 30 existing smoke detectors and add 10 new manual pull stations.

## Materials

- Addressable smoke detector: $95.00 each. Need 30 detectors.
- Manual pull station, addressable: $125.00 each. Need 10 stations.
- Horn/strobe, wall mount: $145.00 each. Need 20 devices.
- Fire alarm control panel, 100 point: $3,200.00 each. Need 1 panel.
- 18 AWG FPLR cable, 1000 ft spool: $185.00 per spool. Need 2 spools.
- Back box, 4-inch square: $3.20 each. Need 50 boxes.

## Labor

- Electrician: 1 hour per device at $79.00 per hour.
- Fire alarm technician: 0.5 hours per device at $88.00 per hour.
