# UPS Backup System Proposal

## Project Summary

Synthetic proposal for a UPS backup system in Raleigh, NC.

## Scope

Install a 20 kVA UPS with external battery cabinet for a server room.

## Materials

- 20 kVA UPS unit: $8,500.00 each. Need 1 unit.
- External battery cabinet: $2,400.00 each. Need 1 cabinet.
- 12 V 9 Ah battery: $85.00 each. Need 40 batteries.
- Battery disconnect switch: $245.00 each. Need 1 switch.
- 2/0 AWG battery cable, 10 ft: $42.00 each. Need 20 pieces.
- Cable lug, 2/0 AWG: $3.50 each. Need 80 lugs.

## Labor

- Electrician: 12 hours at $77.00 per hour.
- UPS technician: 8 hours at $105.00 per hour.
