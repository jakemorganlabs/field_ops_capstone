# Code Reference for 20 kVA UPS and Battery Cabinet in NC

This code reference applies to 20 kva ups and battery cabinet installations in Raleigh, NC. It summarizes the governing requirements for estimating and installation. Synthetic summary for testing only.

## Governing Codes

Code requirements covered: NEC Article 645, runtime requirement. NC code reference.

- NEC Article 645 Information Technology Equipment and Article 480 Storage Batteries
- IEEE 1188 VRLA battery maintenance

## Requirements

- NEC Article 645 permits the UPS within the IT room only when it is rated 750 VA or more with a disconnecting means at the principal exit doors.
- The runtime requirement of 15 minutes at full load must be verified by a discharge test at commissioning.
- Battery cabinet ventilation must limit hydrogen to 1 percent by volume.
- A disconnect switch is required on both the UPS input and output, lockable in the open position.
- 12 V VRLA batteries must be replaced as a full string; mixing ages is not permitted.
- The maintenance bypass must allow UPS service without dropping the critical load.

## Labor Guidance

- Work must be performed by a licensed contractor with a permit and final inspection.
- Inspection and documentation time should be estimated at 5 percent of installation labor.
