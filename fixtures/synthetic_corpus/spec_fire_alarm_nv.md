# Manufacturer Specification: Addressable Fire Alarm Devices Materials Specification

## Overview

Manufacturer specifications and unit costs for addressable smoke detector, manual pull station, horn/strobe, control panel.

This document lists manufacturer materials and unit costs for a addressable fire alarm devices project in Las Vegas, NV. All prices are synthetic and for testing only.

## Materials

Items covered: addressable smoke detector, manual pull station, horn/strobe, control panel.

- Addressable smoke detector, photoelectric, with base: $95.00 each. One per replaced detector.
- Manual pull station, addressable, dual action: $125.00 each. One per exit.
- Horn/strobe, wall mount, 15 to 110 cd selectable: $145.00 each. One per notification zone.
- Fire alarm control panel, addressable, 100 point: $3,200.00 each. One per building.
- 18 AWG FPLR fire alarm cable, 1000 ft spool: $185.00 per spool. One spool per 25 devices.
- Back box, 4 inch square, red: $3.20 each. One per device.

## Labor

- Electrician: 1 hour per device.
- Fire alarm technician: 0.5 hours per device plus 8 hours programming.
