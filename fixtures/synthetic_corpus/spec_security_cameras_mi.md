# Manufacturer Specification: IP Video Surveillance Materials Specification

## Overview

Manufacturer specifications and unit costs for IP dome camera, NVR, hard drive, Cat6 cable, PoE switch.

This document lists manufacturer materials and unit costs for a ip video surveillance project in Detroit, MI. All prices are synthetic and for testing only.

## Materials

Items covered: IP dome camera, NVR, hard drive, Cat6 cable, PoE switch.

- IP dome camera, 4 MP, IR, vandal resistant: $285.00 each. One per camera location.
- Network video recorder, 32 channel: $1,950.00 each. One per 32 cameras.
- Surveillance hard drive, 8 TB: $210.00 each. Four per NVR.
- Cat6 riser cable, 1000 ft box: $165.00 per box. One box per 6 cameras.
- PoE switch, 24 port, 370 W: $640.00 each. One per 24 cameras.
- Camera junction box, outdoor: $28.00 each. One per exterior camera.

- Electrician: 1 hour per camera.
