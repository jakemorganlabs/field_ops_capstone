# Grounding System Proposal

## Project Summary

Synthetic proposal for a grounding system upgrade in Tampa, FL.

## Scope

Install a new ground ring, ground rods, and bonding for a commercial facility.

## Materials

- 4/0 AWG bare copper ground wire, 1000 ft spool: $1,850.00 per spool. Need 1 spool.
- Copper-clad ground rod, 10 ft: $45.00 each. Need 12 rods.
- Ground rod clamp: $8.50 each. Need 12 clamps.
- Exothermic welding kit: $485.00 each. Need 1 kit.
- Ground bus bar: $125.00 each. Need 2 bus bars.
- Anti-oxidant compound: $24.00 each. Need 5 tubes.

## Labor

- Electrician: 24 hours at $76.00 per hour.
- Apprentice: 12 hours at $36.00 per hour.
