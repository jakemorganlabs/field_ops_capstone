# Code Reference for Building Automation and VAV Controls in OR

This code reference applies to building automation and vav controls installations in Portland, OR. It summarizes the governing requirements for estimating and installation. Synthetic summary for testing only.

## Governing Codes

Code requirements covered: ASHRAE 90.1, BACnet protocol. OR code reference.

- ASHRAE 90.1 Energy Standard Section 6.4 HVAC controls
- ASHRAE 135 BACnet protocol

## Requirements

- ASHRAE 90.1 requires each VAV zone to have a thermostat with a 5 degree F deadband and automatic setback.
- Demand control ventilation with a CO2 sensor is required for zones over 500 square feet with occupant density above 25 per 1000 square feet.
- All controllers must communicate over the BACnet protocol; proprietary protocols are not accepted on the building network.
- The MS/TP trunk is limited to 32 devices per segment and 4000 feet of cable.
- Optimum start and supply air temperature reset are required control sequences.
- Point-to-point checkout of every sensor and actuator must be documented at commissioning.

## Labor Guidance

- Work must be performed by a licensed contractor with a permit and final inspection.
- Inspection and documentation time should be estimated at 5 percent of installation labor.
