# EV Charging Station Installation Proposal

## Project Summary

Synthetic proposal for Level 2 EV charging stations in Denver, CO.

## Scope

Install six Level 2 EV charging stations in a commercial parking garage.

## Materials

- Level 2 EV charging station, dual port: $2,450.00 each. Need 6 stations.
- 50 A circuit breaker, 208 V: $85.00 each. Need 6 breakers.
- 6 AWG THHN copper wire, 500 ft spool: $425.00 per spool. Need 2 spools.
- 3/4 inch flexible metal conduit, 100 ft coil: $145.00 per coil. Need 4 coils.
- Bollard mount, steel: $125.00 each. Need 6 bollards.
- Signage, EV parking: $45.00 each. Need 6 signs.

## Labor

- Electrician: 6 hours per station at $78.00 per hour.
- Apprentice: 3 hours per station at $36.00 per hour.
