# Audio Visual System Proposal

## Project Summary

Synthetic proposal for an AV system in Atlanta, GA.

## Scope

Install a conference room AV system with display, speakers, and control panel.

## Materials

- 86-inch 4K display: $2,850.00 each. Need 1 display.
- Ceiling speaker pair: $485.00 each. Need 4 pairs.
- Wireless presentation system: $1,250.00 each. Need 1 system.
- 8-inch touch panel controller: $1,850.00 each. Need 1 panel.
- HDMI over IP encoder/decoder set: $685.00 each. Need 2 sets.
- Wall mount for display: $245.00 each. Need 1 mount.

## Labor

- AV technician: 8 hours at $88.00 per hour.
- Electrician: 4 hours at $76.00 per hour.
