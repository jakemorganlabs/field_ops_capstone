# National Electrical Code Reference (CA Synthetic)

## Article 800 Communications Circuits

Communications cables in plenum spaces must be listed as suitable for the purpose. In California commercial installations, plenum-rated Cat6A cable is required for air-handling spaces.

## Article 300 Wiring Methods

Penetrations of fire-rated assemblies must be firestopped with approved methods. Firestop putty and intumescent sealant are acceptable when installed per manufacturer instructions.

## Article 110.3(B) Installation and Use

Listed or labeled equipment must be installed and used in accordance with any instructions included in the listing or labeling.

## Article 250 Grounding and Bonding

Communications equipment must be grounded in accordance with Article 250. Cable trays and metallic raceways must be bonded to ground.
