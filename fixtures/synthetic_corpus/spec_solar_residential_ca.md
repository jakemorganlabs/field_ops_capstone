# Manufacturer Specification: Residential Rooftop Solar Materials Specification

## Overview

Manufacturer specifications and unit costs for solar panel, string inverter, racking system, PV wire, roof flashing.

This document lists manufacturer materials and unit costs for a residential rooftop solar project in San Diego, CA. All prices are synthetic and for testing only.

## Materials

Items covered: solar panel, string inverter, racking system, PV wire, roof flashing.

- Solar panel, 400 W monocrystalline: $215.00 each. Twenty panels per 8 kW array.
- String inverter, 7.6 kW, with rapid shutdown: $1,850.00 each. One per array.
- Racking system, rail and clamp kit per panel: $48.00 per panel. One kit per panel.
- PV wire, 10 AWG, 500 ft spool: $310.00 per spool. One spool per array.
- Roof flashing, composition shingle, with lag: $14.00 each. One per rail attachment.
- DC and AC disconnect, 60 A: $120.00 each. One of each per array.

## Labor

- Electrician: 12 hours per array.
- Installer: 24 hours per array.

- Region: CA
