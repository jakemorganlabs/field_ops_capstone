# Manufacturer Specification: Card Access Control Doors Materials Specification

## Overview

Manufacturer specifications and unit costs for card reader, electrified strike, door sensor, access control panel.

This document lists manufacturer materials and unit costs for a card access control doors project in Chicago, IL. All prices are synthetic and for testing only.

## Materials

Items covered: card reader, electrified strike, door sensor, access control panel.

- Proximity card reader, mullion mount: $165.00 each. One per controlled door.
- Electrified strike, fail secure, 12/24 V: $210.00 each. One per door.
- Door position sensor, recessed: $18.00 each. One per door.
- Access control panel, 8 door, with enclosure: $2,400.00 each. One per 8 doors.
- Request-to-exit motion sensor: $72.00 each. One per door.
- 22/4 and 18/2 composite access cable, 500 ft: $210.00 per spool. One spool per 4 doors.

## Labor

- Technician: 4 hours per door.
- Electrician: 1 hour per door.
