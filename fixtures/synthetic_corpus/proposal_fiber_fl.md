# Fiber Optic Trunk Proposal

## Project Summary

Synthetic proposal for a fiber optic trunk installation at Kennedy Space Center, FL.

## Scope

Install radiation-hardened fiber optic trunk for orbital solar array telemetry.

## Materials

- Radiation-hardened single-mode fiber cable, 500 ft spool: $1,240.00 per spool. Need 2 spools.
- Space-grade transceiver, 10 Gbps: $485.00 each. Need 4 transceivers.
- Vacuum-rated connector, single-mode: $95.00 each. Need 8 connectors.
- Fiber enclosure, wall mount: $210.00 each. Need 2 enclosures.
- Cable tray, aluminum, 10 ft: $78.00 each. Need 20 pieces.

## Labor

- Fiber technician: 8 hours per run at $85.00 per hour.
- Aerospace technician: 6 hours per run at $95.00 per hour.
