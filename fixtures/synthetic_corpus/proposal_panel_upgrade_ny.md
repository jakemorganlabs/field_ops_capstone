# Electrical Panel Upgrade Proposal

## Project Summary

Synthetic proposal for a service panel upgrade in Buffalo, NY.

## Scope

Replace an existing 100 A service with a 200 A main panel and new feeders.

## Materials

- 200 A main breaker panel, 40 circuit: $485.00 each. Need 1 panel.
- 200 A main breaker: $145.00 each. Need 1 breaker.
- 2/0 AWG copper THHN, 500 ft spool: $1,850.00 per spool. Need 1 spool.
- 4 AWG copper ground wire, 250 ft spool: $245.00 per spool. Need 1 spool.
- 2-inch PVC conduit, 10 ft: $18.50 each. Need 10 sticks.
- 2-inch LB conduit body: $24.00 each. Need 2 bodies.
- Ground bar kit: $32.00 each. Need 1 kit.

## Labor

- Electrician: 16 hours at $82.00 per hour.
- Apprentice: 8 hours at $38.00 per hour.
